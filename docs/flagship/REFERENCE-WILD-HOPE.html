Why we are called that

Hope is not a mood. It is the thing that got us out of bed.

We started in a living room in 2019 with eleven people and a borrowed coffee urn. Nobody had a plan for a building. What we had was a shared suspicion that God was not finished with any of us, and that a room full of honest people was worth more than a good sound system.

Seven years on we are a few hundred, we own the coffee urn, and the suspicion has not changed. Wild, because hope out here is not tidy. Hope, because it turned out to be true.

Before you come

The three things people actually want to know.

What do I wear?

What you wore yesterday. Half the room is in flannel and boots, and nobody has ever been turned away for it.

Will I get singled out?

No. We do not make visitors stand, we do not pass a card down the row, and the offering is a box by the door that you can walk straight past.

What if I do not believe any of it?

Then you are in good company, and you are still welcome. Plenty of people here spent a long year in the back row before anything shifted. There is no clock on it.
