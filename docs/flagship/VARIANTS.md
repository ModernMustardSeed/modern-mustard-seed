# Art directions

Pick ONE and commit to it. A build that hedges between two reads as a template.
`TEMPLATE.md` holds the anatomy and the laws; this holds the look.

State your choice in the `<!--DIRECTION:-->` comment as three words, then let it
govern every decision: type, colour, photography, motion, and the copy register.

## How to choose

Read the trade, the town, and what the owner actually said, in that order. The
direction has to be true about THEM, not a mood you like. A 41-year boutique on
a resort main street is not the same as a second-generation excavation company,
and the two should not be able to swap stylesheets.

Then pick the direction that is hardest to fake. If any competitor in that trade
could ship the same page with their name on it, start again.

## The ten

### 1. Warm considered unhurried
Cream paper, one deep jewel accent, high-contrast serif display, generous air.
Photography is interior, window-lit, tactile. For businesses whose value is
taste and time: boutiques, galleries, tailors, framers, bookshops.
*Reference: `REFERENCE.html`, the Sappari build.*

### 2. Cold steel and daylight
Near-white, graphite, one safety accent. Condensed grotesk, tight leading, hard
rules. Photography is machinery, material and process in flat daylight. For
fabrication, welding, machining, industrial service.

### 3. Deep field
Near-black ground throughout, a single warm light source per section, oversized
display type floated on photography. For evening trades: restaurants, bars,
music venues, event spaces.

### 4. Blueprint
Paper-white, thin rules, mono labels, measured margins, diagrams as decoration.
Photography is site work and finished detail. For architects, surveyors,
engineers, custom builders.

### 5. Field and season
Earth ground, one growing green or harvest gold, humanist serif, wide photo
plates that change with the season. For landscaping, nurseries, farms, orchards,
tree work.

### 6. Chrome and asphalt
Charcoal, one vivid signal colour, italic condensed display, motion that reads
as speed. Photography is vehicles, bays, and road. For auto, towing, detailing,
motorsport.

### 7. Clinic calm
Soft neutral, one cool accent, humanist sans, plenty of whitespace, nothing
shouting. Photography is rooms, light and hands, never stock smiles. For dental,
therapy, veterinary, optometry, med spa.

### 8. Ledger
Ivory, ink, one restrained burgundy or navy, classical serif, rules and columns
that read as a printed document. For attorneys, accountants, notaries, estate
and funeral services.

### 9. Alpine
Cool white and slate with one alpenglow warm, large landscape plates, airy type.
Photography is terrain and weather. For guides, outfitters, lodges, rentals,
resort services.

### 10. Workshop
Kraft and oxide, letterpress feel, slab or wood-type display, texture on every
surface. Photography is the bench, the tool, the maker's hands. For carpentry,
leather, ceramics, custom fabrication, restoration.

## Hero composition modes

Choose one, and let the scrim follow from it (`LEGIBILITY_LAW`).

**Plate.** One full-bleed photograph, copy in a lower corner, gradient scrim
from the copy edge. The default, and what the reference uses.

**Split.** Photograph on one half, solid colour on the other, copy on the solid
side. The safest for legibility and the least atmospheric. Use it when the only
available photography is busy edge to edge.

**Stack.** Oversized wordmark or headline above the photograph, on the page
ground, with the image starting below it. Use when the name itself is the asset.
The trap: leave real clearance between the type and the image, or the descenders
collide with the top edge.

## What is banned in every direction

- drawn SVG scene art anywhere, hero or otherwise
- one photograph repeated into more than one visual slot
- an invented review, rating, year in business, or certification
- their name lettered onto a building, a truck, a sign, or an awning
- text on photography without a scrim
- a bare dot or circle riding the cursor
