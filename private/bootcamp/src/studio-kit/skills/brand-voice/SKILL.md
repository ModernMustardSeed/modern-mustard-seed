# Brand Voice

One voice across every channel, so a customer reading the website, a proposal
and a Tuesday email hears the same person. Fill in the three FILL IN blocks the
first afternoon. Everything else applies as written.

## Who is speaking

FILL IN: the owner's name, the business name, and in one line who the customer
is. Example: "Dana Ruiz, Ruiz Roofing. Homeowners in the valley with a leak
today and a budget they have already thought about."

## How it sounds

FILL IN: three adjectives and one sentence that sounds exactly right. Example:
"Plain, warm, certain. 'We will be there Thursday at eight and the quote is the
price.'"

## Words we use and words we never use

FILL IN: the product names, the retired names, the phrases that are banned.
Mirror the banned ones in studio-kit.json so the words guard enforces them.

## The rules that apply to every business

1. **Lead with the answer.** No warm-up paragraph. The first sentence carries
   the point.
2. **Be specific.** A number, a date, a name, a file. "Soon" becomes "Thursday".
   "Many customers" becomes "41 customers".
3. **No hedging.** "This will fix the leak," not "this should potentially help".
   If it is not certain, find out, then write the sentence.
4. **We, us, our.** The business speaks as itself. A customer quote is the only
   third person allowed.
5. **One apology at most.** Never in a subject line. Never an explanation of an
   internal mistake the customer did not need to know about. Say what is fixed.
6. **Short paragraphs.** Two to four sentences. One idea each.
7. **No filler.** Delete "I hope this finds you well", "just checking in",
   "please do not hesitate". End on the next step.
8. **Prices are stated once, plainly,** in the form the rules file sets. Never
   in a form the rules file bans.

## The check before anything goes out

Read it aloud as the owner. Then:

- Does the first sentence carry the point?
- Is every vague word replaced with a specific one?
- Is there a hedge, an extra apology, a filler line, or a banned word?
- Is there exactly one next step?

Fix what you find and say what you changed in one line.
