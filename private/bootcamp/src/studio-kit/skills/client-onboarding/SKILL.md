# Client Onboarding

The week after a customer says yes decides whether they trust us for the next
year. Onboarding is fast, specific and finished in one pass.

## The packet

1. **The welcome email.** Thank them once, name the package they bought, give
   the kickoff date and say exactly what we need from them by when. Under 150
   words.
2. **The intake questions.** Tailored to their trade and their package. Ten
   questions at most, each one something only they can answer. Never ask for
   what their website already says.
3. **The access checklist.** Every login we need, with who owns it and how to
   share it safely: domain registrar, website host, email provider, business
   listings, social accounts, payment processor, calendar. Each item says how
   to grant access without sending a password.
4. **The scope confirmation.** The deliverables from the proposal, word for
   word, and what is out of scope. One line on how adjustments work, in the
   words the rules file uses.
5. **The schedule.** Dated milestones from kickoff to launch, each with what
   we deliver and what we need from them first.

## Rules

- The customer gets their own accounts in their own name. Our master keys never
  go into a customer's system.
- One customer, one project record. Never two records for the same work.
- Photos and answers they send are saved the day they arrive, in the project
  folder, named so anyone could find them.

## Done means

The packet is sent by the owner, the project record shows every item with its
owner and date, and the kickoff is on both calendars.
