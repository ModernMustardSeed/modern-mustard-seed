# Monthly Client Review

Every customer hears from us once a month with proof of what we did and what
it changed. The owner reads every report before it goes.

## The sweep

For each live customer, read only. Change nothing during the sweep.

1. **What we shipped this month.** From the project record and the commit log,
   in plain words.
2. **What it changed.** The numbers that matter to their business: calls,
   bookings, form fills, reviews, rankings, sales. Each number with its source
   and its date range. Never estimate a number that was not measured.
3. **What broke and what we fixed.** Only what the customer noticed or would
   want to know. One line each.
4. **What comes next.** The one thing we recommend for next month, and why.

## The report

One page. Their name at the top, the month, four sections matching the sweep.
A chart only when a trend matters, with units and a clear start and end.

## The email

Under 150 words. The headline result in the first sentence, the report
attached or linked, one next step. No apology unless something went wrong
that they saw, and then once.

## The review gate

Every report and email waits for the owner. The owner approves, edits or holds
each one. Only approved ones send, and the send guard asks before each.

## Done means

Every live customer has an approved report, every approved email went out, and
the project record shows the date each was sent.
