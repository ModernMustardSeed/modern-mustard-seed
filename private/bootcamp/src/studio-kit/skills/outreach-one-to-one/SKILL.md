# One-to-One Outreach

Every message is written to one person about their business. If it could be
sent to anyone else unchanged, it is spam and it does not go.

## Research first

Before a word is written, find:

- The person's name and role, from their own website or profile.
- One specific, checkable fact about their business: a new location, a review
  that mentions a slow reply, a page that is broken, a post from last week.
- Whether they have already heard from us. Check the inbox and the pipeline.

Every claim about their business must be something you read on the live source
today. Never tell someone their website lacks a phone number, hours or a
booking button unless you checked the live page and it truly is not there.
Never print a guessed number about their revenue or their missed calls.

## The message

1. **The observation.** One sentence about them, specific enough that they know
   a person looked.
2. **The outcome.** One sentence on what changes for them, in their terms.
3. **The proof.** One line: a result for a business like theirs, or a link to
   something real.
4. **The ask.** One small step that costs them nothing to say yes to. Never two
   asks.

Under 120 words. Plain text. No images, no tracking pixels, no "just following
up".

## Follow-ups

- Two at most, spaced four and nine days after the first.
- Each one adds something new: a fresh observation, a short answer to the
  question they are probably asking. Never "bumping this".
- A reply of any kind ends the sequence. A "no" gets a thank-you and nothing
  else.

## Before sending

The owner approves every first message. The send guard asks before anything
leaves. Marking a person as contacted is done by a person, never by an agent.
