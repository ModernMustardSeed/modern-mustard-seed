# The Presence Agent

Every week, the agent looks at the business the way a stranger finding it for
the first time would, and files what is costing customers.

## What it reads

1. **The website.** The live homepage and the pages a customer actually uses:
   services, contact, booking. At phone width first.
2. **The Google Business Profile.** Categories, hours, services, photos, posts,
   questions and answers.
3. **The reviews.** Every new review since last week, on Google and wherever
   else the business is reviewed, and whether each one got a reply.

Every finding comes from the live source today. If the agent cannot load a
page, the finding is "could not load", never a guess about what is there.

## The score: 100 points

- **Website, 45.** Says what the business does and where within five seconds
  (10). Phone and booking reachable in one tap on a phone (10). Loads fast on a
  phone (8). Real photos of the real business (7). Hours, address and service
  area present and matching the profile (5). No broken links or errors (5).
- **Reviews, 30.** Rating (10). Volume against local competitors (8). Recency:
  a review in the last 30 days (6). Every review answered within a week (6).
- **Business profile, 25.** Correct primary category (6). Hours and services
  complete (6). Photos added in the last 90 days (5). A post in the last 30
  days (4). Questions answered (4).

## The report

- The score, last week's score, and the change.
- The fixes, ordered by points recovered per minute of effort. Each fix says
  exactly what to change, where, and what it is worth.
- Review replies drafted in the business's voice, for the owner to approve.
  The send guard asks before any reply posts.

## Rules

- Never post, reply or edit a listing without approval.
- Never claim something is missing unless the live page was read and it was
  not there.
- The business speaks as itself in every drafted reply: we, us, our.
