Putting it in Zoho Mail

  1. Open mail.zoho.com, then Settings (the gear), then Signature.
  2. Edit your signature, select everything already in the box, and delete it.
  3. Paste. The images come from modernmustardseed.com, so they load for everyone.
  4. Set it to appear on new mail and on replies and forwards, then save.

On your phone

  1. Open this page in the phone browser and press Copy signature.
  2. Zoho Mail app, Settings, your account, Signature. Paste and save.
