Our work

We build slowly, on purpose.

Every Cairnfell home starts on the land, not at a desk. We walk the site in every season before we draw a line: where the wind comes from in February, where the sun sits at five o'clock in July, where the snow drifts and where the elk cross. The house answers all of it.

Then our own crews build it. Our log wrights scribe every course by hand. Our timber framers cut every joint in our shop in Columbia Falls and raise it on site with pegs, not brackets. We take on six homes a year, so the people who start your home are the people who finish it.

The build

Four seasons to a front door.

A Cairnfell home takes eighteen to twenty-six months from the first walk of the land to the day we hand you the keys. Here is every stage, and who you'll be standing next to.

  1. Stage 1Months 1–2

    Walking the land

    We visit your site in at least two seasons with our architect and our lead builder. Sun path, wind, snow, soils, views, water, access. If you don't have land yet, we'll walk the parcels you're considering with you before you buy.

  2. Stage 2Months 2–7

    Drawing it together

    Plans, models and a full material library at our Whitefish studio. You'll hold the actual logs, stone and hardware before anything is ordered. We fix a price with you before we break ground.

  3. Stage 3Months 6–12

    In the shop

    Logs are hand-peeled, scribed and test-stacked in our Columbia Falls yard. Timber frames are cut and pre-fit. You're welcome to visit any Friday, and most owners do.

  4. Stage 4Months 10–24

    Raising the home

    The shell goes up in weeks and then the slow work begins: stone, millwork, plaster, forged iron. Your owner portal shows every week of it, photographed by the crew on site.

  5. Stage 5Keys, and after

    Handing it over

    A full walk-through, a bound home manual, and a first-winter visit to check every log, seal and system after the first real cold. We stay your builder for the life of the home.

Questions we hear

Plain answers, first.

How long does a custom log home take in Montana?

Eighteen to twenty-six months from the first walk of the land to the keys. About six of those months are design and permits, four are in our shop while the logs are scribed and test-stacked, and the rest is on your site.

Do you build outside Whitefish and Big Sky?

Yes. We build across the Flathead and Gallatin valleys, including Bigfork, Columbia Falls, Lakeside, Kalispell, Bozeman and the Swan Valley. Outside those, we take a few commissions a decade where the land is right.

Can you help us find land before we buy?

We walk parcels with our owners before they buy, and we tell you plainly what a site will cost to build on: access, well depth, septic, slope, snow load and fire rating. It's the most valuable afternoon of the whole project.

Do you build through the winter?

Yes. Our crews work through snow. Logs and timbers are cut in the shop all winter, and we plan the roof to be dried in before the heavy snow arrives, usually by late November.

How do we follow the build if we live out of state?

Every Cairnfell home comes with a private owner portal: photos and a written update every Friday, the live schedule, selections to approve, and a concierge that answers questions about your build at any hour.
