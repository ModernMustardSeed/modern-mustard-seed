Our story

Two strangers on a sunset sail

We met on a catamaran off Mo'orea in 2021, both on solo trips, both pretending we were not seasick. Marco offered me his ginger candy. I offered him the better seat. Four years, two continents and one very wobbly proposal on a paddleboard later, we are going back to where it started.

Come for the vows, stay for the lagoon. We would love nothing more than to have you with us for all of it.

Good to know

Questions

Do I need a passport?

Yes. French Polynesia is an overseas collectivity of France, so bring a passport valid for your whole stay. U.S. travelers do not need a visa for a short visit.

What do I wear on the sand?

Island formal: linen, silk, light suits, sundresses. Leave the heels at home; the ceremony is barefoot-friendly and flat sandals are perfect.

Can I bring the kids?

Kids named on your invitation are welcome, and there is a sitter and a sandcastle station during dinner.

What if it rains?

Tropical showers pass fast. The ceremony moves under the pavilion and we wait out the cloud. It is warm rain, so nobody minds much.

Is there an app?

Yes. Sign in with your name and the code on your invitation to see your table, your dinner, the ferry times and our shared album.
