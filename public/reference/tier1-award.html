Forty-one years

The same door, the same street, four decades of getting it right.

Forty-one years on one block teaches you what people here actually wear. Wool that survives a Montana February. Linen that earns its keep in July. Pieces chosen one at a time, by hand, by someone who will still be here next season to hear how they wore.

That is the whole idea, and it has not changed since 1985.
