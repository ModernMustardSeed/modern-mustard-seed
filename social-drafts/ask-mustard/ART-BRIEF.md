Six woodcut newspaper spot illustrations. The render script works without them
and upgrades automatically when a spot exists at `art/<key>.png`. Spots are
black ink on pure white; the renderer multiply-blends them onto the newsprint
so the white disappears.

## Rules
- Generate all six in ONE chat session so the style stays consistent.
- **Absolutely no text, letters, numbers, words, or watermarks in the image.**
- Size: square **1024x1024**, single subject centered, wide margins, pure
  white background.
- Save as the exact filenames below, drop into `social-drafts/ask-mustard/art/`.
