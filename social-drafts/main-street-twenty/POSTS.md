# The Main Street Twenty · Set twelve · The Trade Posters

Twenty WPA style trade posters, one per trade, honoring the people who keep
Main Street running. Core emotion: PRIDE. No stats, no pitch: the poster is the
whole post and the giveaway is the funnel. This file mirrors
`data/social-cards.ts`. Edit both or neither.

## The campaign

- **Cadence:** one poster per weekday on our own Page for a month (20 posts).
  In other people's groups, only that group's trade, ever.
- **The engine:** whoever runs the trade comments and gets the full resolution
  file free, by DM or email. Deliver the poster FIRST, then one soft line.
- **The escalators, all free:** shop name set into the poster (five minute
  re-render), custom poster for a trade not in the twenty (same day), wall
  photo feature in the series wrap post.
- **Outbound:** reps may attach a lead's own trade poster to a first touch
  EMAIL or offer it on a live call. Never a cold text. Never assert anything
  about their business we have not checked.
- **Honesty rails:** it is AI art, art directed at the studio, say so when
  asked. No prices anywhere, the money question gets the canned reply.
  The /demos link only goes out when someone asks what we do.

## Outbound pairings (email or live call only)

- First touch to any trade in the twenty: attach their poster. "Made this for
  Montana plumbers this month, yours free, print it as big as you like."
- No reply: the shop name offer. "Want your company name set into it?"
- Warm but busy: the wall photo invite.
- Any trade outside the twenty: make the custom poster BEFORE the first touch.

  what you do and we will draw yours. (Make it. Warmest lead in the set.)

  claim hand painted, never dodge.

  Posters are the thank you. /demos link only when they ask.
