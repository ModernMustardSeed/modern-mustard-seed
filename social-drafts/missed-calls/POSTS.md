## Read this before you post in someone else's group

Most SMB groups auto-remove promo. The framing that survives is **the dare, not
the demo**. You are not selling a voice agent, you are handing them a free thing
to poke at and asking what broke. Lead with `05-break-it`. Save `04-call-it` for
your own Page, your own group, and groups that have an explicit promo day.

Rule of thumb per group: serve for a week before you post one of these. If you
have never commented in a group, your first post there should not be this one.
