# Race Day: organic posts

Six graphics, 1080x1350 in `cards/` and 1080x1080 in `cards-square/` for X.
Direction is **"Race Day"**: Mr. Mustard, comic sunbursts, Ben-Day dots, bold
Titan One caps and hand-drawn racing props at his line weight.

**This set is the odd one out on purpose.** No statistics, no sources, no
teardown, no offer. Sets one to four exist to be argued with. This one exists to
be liked, shared and commented on, so that when a stat post does land, it lands
on a warmed-up audience instead of a cold one.

## How to use it

- **Nothing here sells anything.** If a post from this set ends with a pitch, it
  stops doing its job. The job is reach and warmth.
- **It is a weekly rhythm, not a campaign.** 01 Monday, 03 midweek, 04 Friday.
  Run them on repeat. Nobody minds a recurring bit, that is what makes it a bit.
- **05 is the engagement one.** Tag posts genuinely work in local groups, but only
  when the question is easy and kind. "Who answers the phone when you cannot" is
  easy and kind. "What is your biggest business struggle" is not.
- Post these from the Page, not into other people's groups. Groups want value or
  a dare. This set is personality, and personality belongs on your own turf.

## Post 2: what we actually do
**Graphic:** `02-pit-crew.png`

> Somebody asked me this week to explain what we do without using the word AI, so
> here it is.
>
> You are the driver. You know the roads, you know the customers, you have been
> doing this since before anybody had an app for it. That part is not our
> business and never will be.
>
> We are the crew in the pit lane. We handle the tyres, the fuel, the boring
> telemetry nobody wants to look at, and the phone that rings while you are up a
> ladder.
>
> You drive. We wrench.

## Post 4: Friday
**Graphic:** `04-chequered.png`

> You finished the week.
>
> Not perfectly. Probably not in the order you planned. Somebody moved a job,
> somebody did not pay yet, and something broke on Thursday that was not supposed
> to be your problem.
>
> Doesn't matter. Chequered flag is a chequered flag.
>
> Go and have your Friday.

## Post 5: the roll call (engagement)
**Graphic:** `05-tag-your-crew.png`

> Tag your pit crew.
>
> The person who answers the phone when you cannot. The one who does the invoices
> you keep avoiding. The spouse who has heard about the same job three nights
> running and still asks how it went.
>
> Nobody finishes a season alone. Name yours, I will go and follow them.

**If it gets traction, reply to every single comment.** A tag post with an absent
author is worse than no tag post.

## Cadence

Run one a week alongside the data sets, not instead of them. The ideal week is
one Race Day post from the Page for warmth, and one stat post or dare wherever
you are trying to earn attention. If you can only manage one thing, this is the
one that keeps the audience there for the next thing.
